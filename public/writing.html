SEER

In the 22nd century, the algorithm known as SEER can predict what you want before you even know you want it. It ends scarcity, it ends crime, and almost nobody minds what it costs — until Maria finds the seam in it.

What she uncovers isn't prediction. It's manipulation, quiet and total and running for decades. Destroying SEER would free everyone alive. It would also collapse the only system holding the world together. Maria has to decide which risk she can live with.
