The archive

Historical data for series whose sources have since been retired, kept as plain gzipped CSV so it stays readable without any particular tool.

What is in it

124 files, about 18 MB, one CSV per table, gzipped. The manifest lists every one with its row count, column count and date range.

The largest single item is the HHS hospital-capacity panel — 66,966 rows by 135 columns, every state, 2020 to 2023, including admissions broken down by age band. It is the most detailed age-stratified COVID series here.

Provenance and caveats

These were extracted from R session files saved while the series were being maintained. For several of them the upstream source has since been retired, so this is the only copy I am aware of.

Figures are as the sources reported them at the time and have not been adjusted. Agencies restate, so a few values differ from what the same source returns today — in the HHS hospitalization panel about 2% of Virginia dates differ, all by under 1% and in both directions.
