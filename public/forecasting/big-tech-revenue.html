Big-tech revenue

Revenue for Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta and Microsoft, read out of their SEC filings, annually and by quarter. Built for a question on their combined revenue.

The five do not share a fiscal calendar — Apple’s year ends in September and Microsoft’s in June — so the combined line is blank for any period where they have not all filed. A partial sum drawn on the same axis reads as revenue collapsing.

Big-tech revenue by quarter

The same five by quarter, which is where the seasonality lives — Amazon and Apple both spike every December. The old version of this chart had two false spikes in it: Apple appearing to earn $383bn in a quarter and Microsoft $332bn. Those are the companies’ annual figures, which reached the quarterly series because the old code selected filings by form rather than by how long the reporting period actually was. Quarters here are picked by duration. The fourth quarter is then derived from the annual total, because a 10-K often reports the year without ever stating Q4 on its own.
