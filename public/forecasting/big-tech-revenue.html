SEC filings

Big-tech revenue

Revenue for Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Meta and Microsoft, read out of their SEC filings, annually and by quarter. Billions of US dollars.

The five do not share a fiscal calendar — Apple’s year ends in September and Microsoft’s in June — so the combined line is blank for any period where they have not all filed yet. Quarters are placed in the calendar quarter they end in, which is what lets the five line up; the consequence is that adding four calendar quarters for Apple or Microsoft will not equal their fiscal year.

Big-tech revenue by quarter

The same five by quarter, which is where the seasonality lives — Amazon and Apple both spike every December.

Two notes on the derivation. Quarters are selected by the length of the reporting period rather than by filing type, because a 10-K carries both the twelve-month total and three-month figures, and mixing them puts an annual number in the quarterly line. The fourth quarter is calculated as the annual total minus the three reported quarters, since a 10-K frequently reports the year without ever stating Q4 on its own.
