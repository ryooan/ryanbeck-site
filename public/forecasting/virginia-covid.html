VDH, retired

Virginia COVID

Two series from the Virginia Department of Health. Neither is still updating.

Vaccinations run from March 2021 to September 2023 and are complete for that window — nearly ten million rows, by locality, age group and vaccination status.

Cases by age cover only 17 April to 24 September 2024, which is the whole of what VDH kept when it retired that dataset. For an age-stratified Virginia series without a gap, the HHS hospitalization panel on the respiratory page carries admissions broken down by age band from 2020 to 2023.

Historical · data to 19 September 2023

Virginia COVID vaccinations

Counts rather than percentages, from the state’s open-data platform. The companion file carries the same figures split by age group.

909 rows, latest about 2.9 years ago · source: data.virginia.gov (VDH) · download: va-vaccinations.csv · va-vaccinations-by-age.csv

Virginia COVID cases by age

Cumulative case, hospitalization and death counts by age band. Earlier figures do not appear to be available anywhere: the Internet Archive’s captures of the original endpoint hold about one day of data each, and the CDC’s equivalent series is reported by HHS region rather than by state.

1,771 rows, latest about 1.9 years ago · source: data.virginia.gov (VDH, retired dataset) · download: vdh-cases-by-age-wide.csv · vdh-cases-by-age.csv
