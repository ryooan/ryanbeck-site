Privacy

Updated September 25, 2026.

March Madness

Without signing in, your bracket picks are saved in your browser. Clearing browser storage can remove them. Download a bracket backup if you want a separate copy.

If you sign in with Google, Google provides an account identifier, name, and verified email claim. The app stores your Google account identifier and name, along with brackets you save to your account. It does not store your email address or Google sign-in token. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or other Google content.

A secure session cookie remembers your sign-in for up to 30 days. Saved brackets and their history remain in the database across tournaments. Private encrypted backups support recovery.

MTG feedback

Help Improve submissions are stored privately for review. They include your suggestion, mechanic, any source link you provide, and submission and review details. Do not include personal information in suggestions.

A browser cookie lasting up to one year helps prevent duplicate submissions. The database stores a hash of that identifier. Daily network hashes limit spam; the feedback database does not store raw IP addresses. Clearing cookies or using another browser can reset browser-based limits.

Hosting and external services

Cloudflare hosts the website and its data. Requests and operational logs may contain technical information used to serve the site, prevent abuse, and investigate errors. Google handles account sign-in. MTG card previews load images from Scryfall. External links and services have their own privacy practices.

Retention and questions

Account records, saved brackets, and feedback do not currently have an automatic deletion schedule. Backups may retain earlier copies. To ask about your information or request removal, contact ryanbeckauthor@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the relevant account or submission, but do not send passwords or sign-in tokens.
