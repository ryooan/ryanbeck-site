A Discord bot for gaming groups, with a lab assistant's manners. Free to add to your server.

Igor is in beta. It runs in a handful of servers while I find what breaks. Features may change, and things will occasionally go wrong. If something does, or you have an idea, email ryanbeckauthor@gmail.com.

World of Warcraft

Your characters, in your Discord

  • Character cards with gear, stats and portrait: /wow card.
  • Level-ups announced for the characters your server follows, and a fresh card at milestones (10, 15, 20…).
  • Live deaths, loot and boss kills within seconds, through the free DOINK addon, in every server you choose.
  • Claim your characters, and prove they're yours by signing in with Battle.net.

Built for WoW Forever on US servers first.

Daily games

A morning head-to-head

  • Reads the results people share in your daily-games channel: Wordle, Clues by Sam, Anno, MapTap and others.
  • Each morning, ranks everyone on the day before, with medals, and names who played the most.
  • Links the week's puzzles, so the next one is a tap away.

Around the server

  • Auto-responders: a keyword, a reply, a reaction.
  • A weekly digest of what people played and who hung out in voice (anyone can opt out).

Getting started

  1. Add Igor to your server. It posts a short welcome with the setup steps.
  2. /config channel picks its channels: daily games, level-ups, weekly digest.
  3. WoW players: /wow claim, then /wow doink (guide).
  4. /help lists everything else.

Help, bugs and ideas

Join Igor's Lab, Igor's own Discord server: ask questions in #help, report bugs and suggest ideas in their forums, and hear about new features first. Or use /feedback in any server with Igor; replies come back by DM.

Privacy

Igor keeps only what its features need, deletes activity after 90 days, and never sells or shares anything. Your claimed characters show only in servers you approve, and /forget-me deletes your data everywhere. The details are in the privacy policy and terms of service.

Thanks

Live WoW events come from DOINK by Ryan Flanagan, a free, MIT-licensed addon and companion. Igor reads the posts DOINK sends. Character data comes from Blizzard's API.
