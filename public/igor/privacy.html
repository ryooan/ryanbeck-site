Igor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Igor is a free Discord bot for gaming communities. This page explains what it stores, why, where, for how long, and how to remove it.

What Igor stores

  • Server settings: the channels and options set with /config.
  • WoW characters a server tracks: name, game version and region, and the latest public details from Blizzard's API: level, item level, race, class, faction, guild, equipped gear, last logout time, and a few lifetime statistics from the armory (creatures and critters killed, honorable kills, quests completed and abandoned, food, bandages and potions used, flight paths, hearthstones, dungeons entered, Need rolls, total deaths, deaths to other players, deaths from falling or drowning, waves, and kill counts for each dungeon's main bosses, which become recorded boss kills). Igor uses these for level-up posts, character cards and the daily WoW roundup.
  • Characters you claim with /wow claim: which characters are yours, which is your main, and whether you verified them. Several people can claim the same character until one of them verifies it. Your claims show only in servers you approve: where you claimed, tracked or looked up the character yourself, or where you said yes when Igor asked you by direct message. There they appear in /wow list, /wow mine and next to the character's posts. /wow here changes this for one server, and /wow sharing new_servers:everywhere skips the asking. If someone else claims a character you've claimed, Igor tells you privately how many other accounts claim it (never who), and remembers when it last told you so it doesn't repeat itself more than weekly.
  • Battle.net sign-in, only if you choose it in /wow verify: you sign in on Blizzard's own page, never Igor's, so Igor never sees your password or email. Blizzard then lets Igor read your account's list of WoW characters (names, game versions, realms or surnames, levels) once. Igor uses that access right away and throws it away; it is never stored. The character list is kept only until you confirm or cancel on Igor's page (at most 20 minutes), then deleted. Afterward Igor keeps only which characters you verified, as with any claim. A record that the sign-in link was made and used is deleted after a day.
  • Live WoW events, for players who use the DOINK add-on with Igor: the character name and what happened (a level, loot, a death, a boss kill, a dungeon and party, and similar), with the time. When a DOINK player's boss kill lists someone in their party whose character is claimed and shown in that server, the kill is recorded for that character too, and the post names them. When a death looks like it came from another player, Igor checks that name against Blizzard's public armory and keeps what it finds (level, race, class, faction, guild) to word the post and count repeat killers.
  • DOINK time played, only if your DOINK companion sends its events to Igor (the forward_url line in the download from /wow doink) and you turned on DOINK's time played: for each event that carries it, the character's name, when it happened and the character's total time played at that moment. Not what happened: that still arrives as the live WoW events above. Igor uses it only to measure your WoW playtime exactly. Nothing is kept while game_time is off in /privacy, and then Igor's download doesn't set your companion to send it. Igor also notes which version of DOINK's companion you run (the version your forwarded events report, or the one Igor's download gave you), only to tell you when a newer one is available. And it notes which kinds of DOINK post have arrived from you (level-ups, loot, mounts, screenshots and so on, with when the first came), only to remind you privately if a kind you picked in /wow doink has never arrived, since its box may be unticked in game. And when Blizzard shows one of your characters leveling up or dying in a session but no DOINK post arrived from it, Igor notes that it checked that logout (your Discord ID, the character's name and the logout time, and whether it sent you a note), so it can tell you privately once that DOINK may have stopped posting, and not again for a few days.
  • Steam, only if you run /steam link: your Steam ID, profile name and the date your account was made (for a "years on Steam" post on its anniversary), your recently played games with their total playtime, the achievements you unlock after linking (with how rare each one is), the playtime you gain, hours milestones you pass in a game, and games you complete. Igor reads only what your profile shows publicly, and about once a day notes whether your profile's "Game details" are public (achievements need them), so it can tell you how to show them. Linking is your request for Igor to keep checking your profile, about every 30 minutes, until you unlink. Your Steam activity shows only in servers where you ran /steam link, and /steam posts turns off any kind of post about you.
  • Daily-game results, only in a channel a server picks as its daily-games channel: Igor reads the messages there to find shared game results (Wordle, Connections, Anno, Clues by Sam and similar, including Anno's screenshot card, which it reads with text recognition). It keeps what it read from each message (the game, the score and who posted it) and each day's ranking, to post the daily head-to-head and the week's champions. It doesn't keep the messages themselves.
  • Auto-responders a server sets up with /autoresponse: the keyword, the reply and reactions, who created it, and how often it fires.
  • Activity for weekly stats, only if the server has the feature on and you haven't opted out:
    • which game Discord shows you playing, with start and end times;
    • which voice channel you were in, with join and leave times.
  • Commands: which Igor command you ran, in which server, and when, so Igor's maker can see how many people use it.
  • Feedback you send with /feedback: your message, any screenshot, and the reply, if there is one.
  • Tips: which tip Igor showed under a server's daily roundup, about whom and when (so the same tip isn't repeated too often), and, for the one person a tip was meant for, the private version waiting to be shown under their next Igor command. Tips name nobody in public.
  • Igor's ledger: running totals per year, so Igor can post a year in review and show your year, five years or all time with /stats after the detailed records above are deleted. Totals only: hours per game and in voice, puzzles played and won, your characters' levels, deaths (and how many were to other players), bosses, epic loot, quests, mounts, duels won, item levels and gear upgrades, your Steam hours, achievements, perfect games and milestones, and how many Igor commands you ran. Some character totals are counted from Blizzard's API (levels and gear seen on the armory); only the counts are kept, never the details behind them. A day is added to the ledger two days after it ends.

Igor reads messages only for three things: the DOINK posts sent to Igor's own webhook; the daily-games channel a server chose; and, in servers with auto-responders, each message is checked against those keywords at the moment it's sent and then forgotten.

Where

Igor runs on a server in the United States (Google Cloud, Iowa), and its database is in the United States (Turso, on Amazon Web Services in Ohio). Turso keeps its own short-term recovery copies of the database, which age out on its schedule.

How long

  • Blizzard data (character details, gear, lifetime statistics and how much they grew each day, item icons, logout times, level-up history, and other players' armory details in PvP deaths): refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as Blizzard's API terms require. A character Blizzard no longer has, or that a server stops tracking, is deleted with its history. The ledger's yearly counts are kept as described below.
  • Activity, live WoW events and Steam activity: deleted after 90 days.
  • DOINK time played: deleted after 45 days, and with your DOINK link (deleted in Discord, /forget-me, or its server removing Igor). The kinds of DOINK post seen from you go with your DOINK link too, as do the logouts Igor checked for missing DOINK posts (also deleted after 30 days).
  • Commands: deleted after 90 days.
  • Daily-game results: deleted after 30 days.
  • Feedback: deleted after a year.
  • Tips: a waiting private tip after a week; the record of tips shown after a year.
  • Your Steam link and claims: until you remove them.
  • Ledger totals: kept until you, or the server, remove them (see below).
  • Notes for Igor's maker: when Igor joins or leaves a server, someone first claims a character or sets up DOINK, or someone's DOINK seems to have stopped posting, Igor's maker gets a private message saying so. Igor keeps only a count of these, for 90 days.
  • Backups: Turso's recovery copies, on Turso's schedule.
  • Server removal: removing Igor from a server deletes that server's data, its ledger totals included. Feedback sent from that server stays (it's the sender's), without the server.

Your choices

  • /privacy lets you turn off each kind of reporting on its own, in every server, or all of them with everything:false: game_time (your games and voice time aren't recorded), puzzles (your daily-puzzle results are left out of the rankings), steam (your Steam isn't posted or shown in roundups, digests or reviews; while you stay linked, Igor keeps reading your profile as before, so turning it back on picks up where you left off, and /steam unlink removes everything), doink (posts from your DOINK are removed and not copied, and you aren't named in or credited from other players' party posts) and wow_name (you aren't named next to WoW characters or on their cards). Run it with no options to see your settings. Characters a server tracks still get their public level-up posts. With puzzles off, results you post in a games channel are still read but left out of the rankings.
  • /steam unlink stops Igor checking your Steam profile and deletes everything it stored about it, including your Steam totals in the ledger.
  • /wow unclaim removes a claim. Verifying with Battle.net leaves nothing to unlink: Igor keeps no access to your account.
  • /forget-me deletes, everywhere: your activity, the characters you added or claimed, your Steam link and activity, your commands, your feedback, your ledger totals, your entries in recent daily-game results, and, for characters you verified with Battle.net, their live WoW events and ledger totals too. (A claim that wasn't verified doesn't prove a character is yours, so it can't erase that character's history.) Any /privacy opt-outs are kept, so Igor keeps honoring them. Copies in Turso's recovery backups age out on its schedule.
  • To ask for anything else to be deleted, email the address below.

Sharing

Igor's data is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Igor sends requests to two outside services:

  • Blizzard's API: character and item lookups (an item's level, for loot posts), which contain nothing about you, and, if you sign in with Battle.net, the one read of your character list described above.
  • Steam's Web API, only for profiles linked with /steam link: reads of the public games, playtime and achievements described above; and, in servers that turn on patch notes, the public announcements of games their players played lately (nothing about anyone is sent).

Igor's sign-in pages (igor.ryanrobertbeck.com) are served through Cloudflare, which sees the visit like any website host. Screenshots in a daily-games channel are downloaded from Discord itself and read on Igor's own server; they aren't sent anywhere else.

Game data is provided as is

World of Warcraft data comes from Blizzard Entertainment and Steam data from Valve. Both are shown as they come, with no promise from Igor, Blizzard or Valve that they are complete, accurate or current. Igor isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment or Valve.

Children

Igor is not meant for anyone under 13, which is also Discord's minimum age.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and big changes are announced in Igor's Lab.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: email ryanbeckauthor@gmail.com, or use /feedback.
