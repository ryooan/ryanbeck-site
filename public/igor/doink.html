Live WoW events in Discord, with Igor and DOINK

Igor posts your WoW Forever level-ups, deaths, loot and boss kills in Discord a few seconds after they happen, in its lab-assistant voice. That takes two pieces from DOINK, a free WoW addon:

  • The DOINK addon notices what happens in the game.
  • The DOINK companion, a small program on your PC, does the posting. WoW addons aren't allowed to use the internet, so something outside the game has to send the events. It runs on Windows, or Linux (Proton, Wine or Lutris). There's no Mac version.

On a Mac, or don't want the companion? After playing, attach your DOINK.lua file with /wow upload in Discord and Igor posts what happened. Level-ups also post on their own a few minutes after you log out, without DOINK.

  1. Get your link from Igor

    In your Discord server, type /wow doink. Igor replies privately with your own DOINK link. Keep it to yourself: anyone with it can post as you. You only ever need one link, even for several servers.

  2. Install the DOINK addon

    From CurseForge (or the CurseForge app, WowUp, or any addon manager), for WoW Forever.

    DOINK on CurseForge

  3. Download the DOINK companion

    Easiest (Windows): press Download DOINK under Igor's /wow doink reply. Igor sends you, privately, DOINK's companion with your link already inside (taken from DOINK's own release and checked against its published checksum). Unzip it, open DOINK\Companion, run doink.exe, and skip step 4.

    Or download it yourself. The companion isn't on the addon sites, and DOINK's newest release doesn't always include it (sometimes only the addon is updated). These buttons point at the newest release that does:

    Companion for Windows (v0.17.0) Linux (v0.17.0)

    Or browse all DOINK releases: the companion is in the larger DOINK-v…zip (about 3.5 MB), not the small -forever.zip, which is the addon alone.

    Unzip it, move the Companion folder somewhere permanent, like Documents\DOINK, and run doink.exe. A gold D appears in your system tray (click ^ by the clock if it's hidden). If Windows warns about an unrecognized app, choose More info → Run anyway.

    Recommended: right-click the gold D and tick Start with Windows. The companion doesn't start with WoW, and nothing posts while it's closed.

  4. Give the companion your link (if you downloaded it yourself)

    Right-click the gold D → Settings, paste your link into the webhook box, and press Send test. A test post appears in Discord.

    Or set it in game with /doink webhook <your link> followed by /reload. The companion only sees a link set in game after a reload or logout.

  5. Choose what to post

    Every event type starts off. In game, press Esc → Macros → New, paste this, and click the macro once. It also clears any link set in game earlier, so the companion's link is used:

    /doink webhook clear
/doink webhook here clear
/doink enable level_up
/doink enable death
/doink enable loot
/doink enable boss_kill
/doink realtime on
/doink realtime image off
/reload

    Also available: quest, skill_up, mount, duel, battleground. realtime on posts within seconds; you'll see the game's "Screen captured" message, which is how DOINK hands events to the companion.

    Screenshots (optional, off by default): change the second-to-last line to /doink realtime image on, or press 📸 Add screenshots to the macro under /wow doink. Each post then includes a picture of your screen at that moment, showing whatever is on it, including chat, whispers, party and guild chat, in every server where your DOINK posts appear.

    Check it: /doink test levelup, then /doink flush. A test post appears in Igor's voice.

  6. More servers, and what each one shows

    Run /wow doink in any other server with Igor, and your events post there too, with no new link. Under that reply, a private menu picks which events each server shows, and buttons move them to another channel or stop them. The events you enable in game are the most any server can show.

Something not working?

  • Nothing posts: is the gold D in your tray? Did Send test post? If you set the link in game, did you /reload?
  • Posts only when you log out: type /doink realtime on.
  • Lots of "Screen captured" messages: that's normal with realtime on. The companion deletes those screenshots after reading them and never touches your own.
