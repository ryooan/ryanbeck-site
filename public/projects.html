Projects

Mostly data that needed watching. Each of these is a live dashboard that rebuilds itself on a schedule — a GitHub Action runs the R, pulls from the source agency, and republishes the page — because forecasting a number is much easier when you are not also re-downloading it by hand every week.

Draft — pulled from the archive to look over. Prune anything stale, and the thumbnails are still placeholder art.

Avian Influenza Depopulations

Birds culled in the ongoing HPAI outbreak, parsed out of the USDA's confirmed-detections tables and plotted over time. Rebuilt automatically as new detections are posted.

Public healthUSDAR

US Debt Limit

Treasury's daily cash balance and outstanding debt against the statutory ceiling, straight from the Daily Treasury Statement. Built to forecast X-date questions without re-reading a spreadsheet every morning.

FiscalTreasuryR

US Refugee Admissions

Monthly and fiscal-year refugee admissions from the State Department's Refugee Processing Center, against the annual presidential ceiling.

Influenza Hospitalizations

State-level flu hospital admissions, assembled for the Metaculus FluSight forecasting challenge so the numbers were ready before the questions closed.

EpidemiologyMetaculusR

Weekly Influenza

National flu hospitalisations from the CDC's FluSight target data, updated on the CDC's own weekly cadence.

EpidemiologyCDCR

Weekly COVID

US COVID hospitalisations from the COVID-19 Forecast Hub's truth data — the series the hub scores its forecasts against.

EpidemiologyForecast HubR

COVID Hospital Admissions

Weekly confirmed COVID admissions for Virginia alongside the national trend.

Adult & Pediatric Admissions

Total weekly hospital admissions for confirmed COVID, split adult and paediatric, from HealthData.gov.

FAAMG Revenues

Quarterly revenue for the big five US tech firms, kept current for forecasting questions about their combined top line.

Doses administered and population coverage across Virginia, built during the rollout when the state's own dashboard lagged.
