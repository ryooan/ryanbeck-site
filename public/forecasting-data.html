Forecasting data

Numbers I kept reaching for while forecasting, collected so I would stop re-downloading them by hand. Each series below refreshes itself on a schedule, and each one states the date its data actually ends — which is not always the date it last ran.

That distinction is the point. A scheduled job reporting success tells you it fetched something, not that anyone upstream is still publishing. One of these pages ran green every ten hours for two years while showing a series that had stopped; the status line on each chart now comes from the data itself, so it cannot make that claim.

US debt limit

Federal debt subject to the statutory ceiling, from Treasury's Daily Treasury Statement, against the ceiling itself. The shaded stretches are the eight periods when Congress suspended the limit rather than raising it — during a suspension there is no number to plot, so the ceiling line stops.

Influenza admissions, state by state

The same weekly flu admissions, split by state and shown as a rate per 100,000 residents. Every panel shares one vertical scale, so the boxes are directly comparable. The rate rather than the raw count is what makes that readable: on raw admissions Texas out-tops Hawaii fifty-five-fold and most of the grid flattens into unreadable slivers, where per head the spread is about four-fold and each state’s season has a visible shape.

11,976 rows, latest 50 days ago · source: CDC FluSight forecast hub · download: flu-hospitalizations-states.csv

Historical · data to 24 September 2024

Virginia COVID cases by age

Five months, and that is all there is. VDH retired this dataset and kept only 2024 — everything before April of that year is gone from the source. Before publishing this as a stub I checked the Internet Archive (its captures hold about one day of data each), the CDC's equivalent (region-level, not state), the datastore behind the retired set (73,920 rows and no more), and every R workspace on my own machine. It is genuinely gone. For an age-stratified Virginia series with no hole in it, the HHS panel above carries age-banded admissions from 2020 to 2023.

1,771 rows, latest about 1.9 years ago · source: data.virginia.gov (VDH, retired dataset) · download: vdh-cases-by-age-wide.csv · vdh-cases-by-age.csv

The archive

Several of these series outlived the places they came from. Where a source has gone for good the history is kept here rather than lost with it — 124 files of it, pulled out of old R session files that were, for a few of these, the only surviving copy.

Browse the archive manifest — every file with its row count and date range.
